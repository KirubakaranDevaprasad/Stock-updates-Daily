How the setup score works

Seven parts, out of 100. Growth (revenue and earnings, year over year) · Profitability (margin, return on equity) · Valuation (forward P/E, and whether earnings are expected to rise) · Balance sheet (debt to equity, current ratio) · Analysts (average target vs price, consensus rating) · Momentum (price vs 50- and 100-day averages) · News tailwind (how each linked theme's driver moved over the last month, times that stock's sensitivity to it).

Missing data scores as neutral, so a thinly covered small cap won't get punished for gaps, and won't get rewarded for them either.

Research tool, not advice. Scores are a mechanical screen built from public data. They aren't buy or sell recommendations, and they can't see what the market already expects. Stocks under $10 can swing hard and trade thinly. Check the filings, size positions sensibly, and talk to a licensed advisor before you invest.
